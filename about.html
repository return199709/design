About

Herbal is a small shop for the dried roots, seeds and berries that go into a Chinese soup pot.

Every ingredient is organically grown and sourced to a very high grade, from the mountain country of rural China — Gansu, Ningxia, Sichuan, Fujian and the other regions these herbs have always come from.

Eleven of them are written up in the Journal: what each one is, where it grows, and what people have long used it for. The same eleven are in the Shop.
